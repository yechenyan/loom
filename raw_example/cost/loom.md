source: https://github.com/PyPSA/technology-data

Example cost modifications for selected technologies in 2040, 2045, and 2050. Each CSV row records one parameter override for one technology and keeps the value, unit, provenance, and any extra explanation together.

Columns:
- technology: technology or asset whose assumption is being updated
- parameter: cost or technical parameter being modified
- value: numeric value assigned to that parameter
- unit: unit for `value`
- source: provenance of the assumption
- further description: extra qualifier, conversion note, or explanatory comment
